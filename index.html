And one more thing before you arrive: install Vernier Spectral Analysis on the laptop you are bringing. It is free, and there are versions for Windows, Mac, iPad and Android. In the lesson, and afterwards

Once you have your readings, these turn them into a graph and an answer.

Excel workbook
Where your results go

Type your absorbance readings into the yellow cells on the Your data tab. The graph, the line of best fit, the gradient and R² all appear on their own. There is also a worked example to check yourself against, and a tab explaining how to build the chart in Excel.
R script · optional
starch_curve.R

Does the same analysis as the workbook, in one click. Save it in the same folder as your workbook, open it in RStudio and press Source. You do not need this to complete the practical.
